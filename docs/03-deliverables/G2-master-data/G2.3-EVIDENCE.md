## Objective
Create one canonical supplier identity that can be referenced by Materials and the later Purchasing module while preserving external-system identities and avoiding duplicate supplier truth.

## Persistence
G2.3 adds:

- `md_supplier`;
- `md_supplier_source_mapping`;
- `md_supplier_article`.

## Canonical supplier identity
`md_supplier` contains:
- stable canonical supplier code;
- legal name;
- optional trade name;
- broad supply category;
- active state;
- creation/update timestamps.

Allowed broad categories:
- UNKNOWN;
- PRODUCT;
- PROCESS;
- SERVICE;
- MIXED.

This field represents what the supplier broadly provides. It is not the purchasing evaluation/rating category.

## External-system identity
`md_supplier_source_mapping` preserves source identity independently from the canonical supplier.

Each mapping stores:
- external system;
- external code;
- source name;
- raw source category/rubro when available;
- mapping status;
- active state;
- approval metadata;
- last-seen timestamp.

Uniqueness is enforced by:
`external_system + external_code`.

A canonical Supplier may therefore be represented in multiple systems such as Prolowin and CRM without creating duplicate suppliers.

## Duplicate/consolidation boundary
Mapping states:
- `MAPPED`;
- `REVIEW_REQUIRED`;
- `REJECTED`.

A `REVIEW_REQUIRED` source can exist without a supplier_id.

This is deliberate: an importer may flag a possible duplicate or candidate match without silently consolidating it. Human/data-governance resolution occurs later.

A `MAPPED` source must point to an existing Supplier and receives approval metadata.

## Supplier ↔ Article sourcing
`md_supplier_article` establishes sourcing master data:
- Supplier;
- Article;
- supplier-specific article code;
- preferred flag;
- lead time in days;
- minimum order quantity using Decimal semantics;
- optional canonical order UoM;
- active state.

One baseline sourcing relationship is allowed per Supplier + Article pair.

If an order UoM is provided it must be an active canonical UoM. G2.3 does not infer conversions between order UoM and Article consumption UoM.

## Purchasing boundary
G2.3 intentionally does **not** own:
- purchase orders;
- price history;
- authorization workflow;
- receipts;
- receiving inspection;
- supplier performance scores/evaluations;
- annual evaluation records.

Those remain responsibilities of the future Purchasing/Quality operational modules.
