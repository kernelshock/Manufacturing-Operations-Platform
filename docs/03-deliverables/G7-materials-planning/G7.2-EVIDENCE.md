## Source-of-truth and mathematical rules

For each **active location** of the selected plant:

`physical = sum(G6.2 immutable signed movements)`, or UNKNOWN if no opening/movement exists.

`held = sum(G6.5 signed HOLD and RELEASE)`, independent of physical ISSUE.

`unallocated_physical = physical - held`, provided physical is KNOWN. This is **not Quality-released stock nor ATP**.

`quality_usable = UNKNOWN`, `quality_unallocated = UNKNOWN` because G6 has no verified Quality usable/batch state feeding this projection; none is inferred.

`confirmed_in_transit = UNKNOWN`. Manual Prolowin receipt transcription is not proof of physical stock. Verified open order samples are separately shown as `ordered - verified reported receipts` and are not added to physical stock, usable stock, or confirmed in-transit.

Each original Prolowin promise stays immutable, later verified reschedules are separate, and each partial receipt remains attributable to its source. Historical `evidence_cutoff_at` filters *procurement evidence only*.

## Critical limits — do not overstate G7.2

1. **Current-stock snapshot, not historical or time-phased inventory projection.** `snapshot_at` is the current read time; historical `evidence_cutoff_at` does not reconstruct the operational stock at that date. Published G4 dated demand and forecast future inventory consumption require a subsequent controlled time-bucket projection; do not label this as completed MRP.
2. **No Quality availability integration yet.** For even fully initialized physical stock, Quality-usable values remain NULL/UNKNOWN. Consequently the endpoint makes no "ready to use" promise.
3. **Legacy purchasing evidence lacks verified plant assignment.** Open order sample is a **global article-level, manually captured, partial list**, not validated inbound for the selected plant. Its display alongside a plant-scoped physical view must never be interpreted as a plant-specific incoming quantity. It is intentionally not used for net available stock.
4. **No complete Prolowin synchronization**: manual/screenshot captures can omit older POs, deliveries, claims or schedule changes. Absence of source records means UNKNOWN, not zero global backlog.
5. The result is a **read-only source snapshot**, not a reorder-point calculation, PP, CR, ATP, purchase proposal, auto supplier switch, inventory posting or production deployment.
6. Quality owner, supplier-to-plant mapping, verified inbound transit status, G4 dated demand and business-day calendars remain dependencies for future time-phased planning. These need explicit evidence contracts, not guessed values.
7. Tests run only with disposable PostgreSQL and generated synthetic fixtures. No FAIC live inventory or Prolowin data accessed.
