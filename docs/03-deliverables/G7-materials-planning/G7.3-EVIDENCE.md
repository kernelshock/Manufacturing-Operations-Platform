## Business semantics

`actual_lead_business_days = count(enabled local workdays after original PO event date through the final recorded receipt date)`. Only a **fully delivered** verified original line receives actual lead time. PO issue vs supplier acknowledgement is not disambiguated in current legacy source data, so the calculation is a **measured PO-event-to-receipt proxy**, not verified supplier acceptance-to-receipt lead time.

`original_delay_business_days = max(0, workdays(original promised date, final receipt date))` for completed lines. `revised_delay_business_days` uses the latest separately recorded verified revised promise when present, **without altering the original**.

For still-open orders, `actual_lead_time_business_days = NULL`; the original/revised `LATE` status can be evaluated against `as_of`, but no unobserved final receipt date or eventual delivery time is invented.

A completion-sample on-time ratio counts only fully delivered recorded PO lines; open lines do not count as on-time. Mean and dispersion use completed lines only; for one completed record, dispersion remains UNKNOWN (`NULL`).

`PARTIAL_VERIFIED_MANUAL_SAMPLE` means individual transcriptions are independently verified, **not** that Prolowin coverage is complete. Even 100% on-time within a captured subset cannot establish global supplier reliability. Statistical reliability remains `INSUFFICIENT_COVERAGE` until independently verified population coverage and representative sample-size thresholds exist.

## Explicit operational constraints

1. A **plant manufacturing calendar** is used strictly as an analytical **proxy**, not as proof of the supplier's contractual working calendar. It must be ACTIVE, plant-scoped, time-zone identified, effective for the analyzed dates, with explicit weekly windows and exceptions. Partial-day CLOSED exceptions fail closed rather than being silently treated as complete closure.
2. The current manual Prolowin procurement model does not assign every verified PO line to a confirmed plant. Article/supplier samples cannot be interpreted as plant-specific inbound supply.
3. Verified records can omit historic orders, partial receipts, promises and claims; absence is UNKNOWN, not zero events across ERP.
4. Real statutory holiday calendars and supplier/plant calendar versions must be configured and validated before operational procurement forecasts; no country holiday was guessed.
5. SOURCE-SAMPLE actual lead-time and punctuality do **not** by themselves authorize a variability-derived safety-stock calculation. G7.4 must explicitly gate coverage, minimum sample size, consumption representativeness, and Quality/warehouse usable stock.
6. Does not recalculate FAIC inventory, move stock, reserve materials, approve suppliers, change a purchase promise or create an ERP purchase order.
7. No time-phased future ATP/MRP model has been delivered. G7.2's full future-dated material position remains OPEN until validated demand calendars, Quality availability and truly confirmed supply dates exist.
8. Production-risk escalation and alternate-supplier consideration remain audit-backed advisories; never switch suppliers or issue POs automatically.
9. The CI only validates generated synthetic data and test-specific database. Real FAIC production deployment remains unauthorized.
