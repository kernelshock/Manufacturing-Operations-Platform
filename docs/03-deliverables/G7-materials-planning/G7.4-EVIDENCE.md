## Formula contracts and decision rules

Define `d` as **one selected, traceable, nonnegative canonical-UoM consumption rate per business day**, and `L` as **positive business-day lead time**. The lead-time value must be provided explicitly, not silently inferred from a calendar or from missing Prolowin history.

`lead_time_demand = d × L`

**PERCENTAGE:**

`safety_stock = lead_time_demand × safety_percentage`; default `0.25` from G7.1, configurable within the existing G7.1 0–1 range.

`PP = lead_time_demand + safety_stock`.

**VARIABILITY (provisional model, explicit independence assumption):**

`safety_stock = z × sqrt(L × sigma_d² + d² × sigma_L²)`.

`PP = lead_time_demand + safety_stock`.

`sigma_d` is an independently supported daily material demand standard deviation, `sigma_L` is a supported supplier lead-time standard deviation in business days, and `z` is an explicitly approved service factor; this mathematical model does not infer `z` from missing user policy. Supplier history must have **VERIFIED representative coverage**, verified lead time, positive `z`, and at least **12 independent observations of demand and 12 completed supplier orders** (higher configurable minima supported). Any missing/partial or insufficient evidence blocks the VARIABILITY calculation. `Decimal` square root is used with no float conversions.

**Consumption bases:** `PER_PRODUCT` or `PER_SEAT` may supply separately normalized daily material consumption rates. `BOTH` is strictly a comparison mode. If multiple rates are present, the user or governed source must explicitly choose a `source_key`: the rates are **never summed**, avoiding double counting shared Moonraker/Apollo materials or duplicate per-seat/per-product representations. Duplicate source keys or cross-plant/article/UoM rates are rejected.

**Readiness:** Reuses G7.1 `assess_planning_readiness`. Unknown G6 physical stock, unknown Quality-usable amount, unverified calendar, unknown supplier lead time or source coverage, missing demand or lead-time rate, conflicting source identity or incomplete variability evidence make the point BLOCKED and numerically NULL. Partial verified history in percentage method permits only a clearly labeled PROVISIONAL result; never a silently approved purchasing trigger. Known physical zero / known Quality zero and explicit zero demand remain numerical zero, not UNKNOWN.

**No float inputs, no silent rounding.** Existing G7.1 constraints prohibit automated purchase-order issuance. G7.5 will govern any CR calculation or purchase proposal.

## Important scope limits and residual obligations

1. **There is no user-facing G7.4 API or dashboard yet.** This milestone implements deterministic pure policy contracts and tests. G7.7 will expose governed user interfaces, and G7.5 can incorporate scenario review into CR.
2. Daily material demand rates are **explicitly supplied, traceable input scenarios**, not yet a normalized historical consumption series automatically generated from every G6 MES consumption/BOM/material-sharing record. G7.4 does not claim to have ingested, reconciled, deduplicated or validated all FAIC historical consumption.
3. G7.3's manually captured Prolowin evidence is explicitly `PARTIAL_VERIFIED_MANUAL_SAMPLE` with unknown population coverage. It is **not sufficient** for VARIABILITY without an independently verified representative dataset. Completed sample mean/std alone cannot unlock this path.
4. The formula's independence and stationarity assumptions require statistical review before operative use; the 12-observation floor is a guard, **not proof of representative demand or supplier histories**.
5. Source-specific business-day calendars and supplier acknowledgement vs PO issue must be defined and verified; G7.3 currently uses an active plant manufacturing-calendar proxy. A 15-day planning horizon is not automatically a supplier's proven lead time.
6. No live Quality usable stock source or time-phased future inventory planning is integrated. Existing G7.2 deferred full time-phased availability, plant-specific supplier inbound and G4 dated demand remain OPEN prerequisites for real purchase recommendations.
7. This implementation has no new database migrations, production-data writes, automated ordering, supplier substitution, physical stock mutation or changes to `main`. No FAIC production deployment is authorized.
8. No implicit rounding to supplier order multiples or minimum order quantities: that belongs in G7.5 after authoritative supplier terms are available.
