## 1. Delivered contract scope

- `src/mop/planning/governance.py`: frozen `ExceptionCase`, `SourcingAlternative`, `ProductionRiskEvidence`, `GovernanceEvent`, `GovernanceProjection`. Deterministic risk severity classification and replay of append-sequenced decisions, with case identity/revision/temporal invariants.
- Four severity grades: WATCH, SHORTAGE, PRODUCTION_RISK, STOPPAGE. An UNKNOWN/PARTIAL impact cannot be classified as WATCH. Real production risk requires source identifiers corresponding to PCP, G4, MES or Production; no simulated inferred stoppages from provisional PP/CR.
- Decision states: OPEN → UNDER_REVIEW → APPROVED_FOR_MANUAL_ACTION / REJECTED → CLOSED. Once decided, subsequent approvals/rejections are invalid.
- Three-party process in contract: identified case creator (Planner/Purchasing/Production Manager), independently identified reviewer (Purchasing/Production Manager/Quality/Direction) and **distinct Direction decision actor**. A decision must contain a reason and approving event requires source evidence references.
- The status `APPROVED_FOR_MANUAL_ACTION` is **not** a PO authorization/creation, not a supplier reassignment and not Quality release.
- Alternative suppliers: must differ from primary, have terms evidence, qualification and capacity separately VERIFIED, each with source identifiers. Otherwise approval fails closed.
- A proposal in G7.5 status BLOCKED or PROVISIONAL cannot receive a positive approval; it may be rejected. Any missing source evidence cannot be silently upgraded by review.
- Event replay accepts monotonic timestamps, sequential revision numbers and matching case IDs, and refuses duplicate submit, unauthorized closure and decision replay following terminal states.
- `tests/g7/test_governance.py`: synthetic examples of production shutdown, shortage, supplier alternatives, independent sign-off, duplicate/out-of-order decisions and no-PO/no-inventory/no-supplier-switch invariants.
- No new database migrations, production network changes or automatic purchase endpoints.
