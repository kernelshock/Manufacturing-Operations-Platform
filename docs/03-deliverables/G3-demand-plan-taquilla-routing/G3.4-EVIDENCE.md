## Binding model
Routing evidence is stored on `TaquillaProductLine`:
- `routing_revision_id`;
- `routing_binding_mode`;
- `routing_effective_at`;
- `routing_override_reason`;
- `routing_bound_at`;
- `routing_bound_by`.

Binding modes:
- `RESOLVED`;
- `OVERRIDE`.

Database CHECK constraints require binding metadata to be either entirely absent or complete. OVERRIDE requires a reason; RESOLVED cannot carry an override reason.

## Automatic resolution
Resolution follows:
`TaquillaProductLine.article_id → ArticleRevision.article_id → RoutingRevision.article_revision_id`.

At the requested effective timestamp, candidates must:
- belong to an ArticleRevision of the exact same product-line Article;
- have RoutingRevision status `ACTIVE`;
- contain the timestamp within `valid_from <= at < valid_to` or open-ended valid_to.

Outcome:
- zero candidates → not found;
- exactly one candidate → bound as RESOLVED;
- more than one candidate → rejected as ambiguous.

The system does not guess which ArticleRevision or route is intended.

## Explicit override
An operator with `demand.edit` may bind a specific RoutingRevision through OVERRIDE.

Override does **not** bypass governance:
- the routing must exist;
- its ArticleRevision must belong to the product-line Article;
- routing status must be ACTIVE;
- routing must be effective at the requested timestamp.

Additional provenance:
- mandatory non-empty reason;
- binding actor UUID;
- routing effective timestamp;
- actual binding timestamp.

Thus override means explicit disambiguation/selection, not permission to use an invalid route.

## Lifecycle
Routing may be bound or rebound only while the parent Taquilla is `DRAFT`.

Once the Taquilla is READY, COMPLETED or CANCELLED, route binding is frozen with the demand definition.
