## Domain model

### ProductionPlan
Owns:
- stable UUID identity;
- editable business `plan_number`;
- optional description;
- lifecycle status;
- created/updated timestamps.

Allowed lifecycle:
`DRAFT → OPEN → COMPLETED`

Cancellation:
- DRAFT → CANCELLED;
- OPEN → CANCELLED.

COMPLETED and CANCELLED are terminal.

The editable business number is not the canonical database identity. Changing a plan number preserves the UUID and every dependent reference.

### Taquilla
Owns:
- stable UUID identity;
- parent ProductionPlan;
- globally unique `taquilla_code`;
- positive `sequence_no`;
- optional description;
- lifecycle status;
- created/updated timestamps.

One Plan may contain multiple Taquillas. Sequence is unique within the Plan.

Allowed lifecycle:
`DRAFT → READY → COMPLETED`

Cancellation:
- DRAFT → CANCELLED;
- READY → CANCELLED.

COMPLETED and CANCELLED are terminal.
