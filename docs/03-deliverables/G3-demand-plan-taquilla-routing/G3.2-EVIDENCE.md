## Domain model
New entity:
`TaquillaProductLine`

Identity and relationships:
- stable UUID line identity;
- parent `Taquilla`;
- exact G2 `Article` reference;
- positive explicit `sequence_no`;
- positive Decimal(24,8) `requested_quantity`;
- optional `source_line_key`;
- created/updated timestamps.

## Master Data boundary
A composition line may reference only an existing, active G2 Article whose `article_kind` is `FINISHED_PRODUCT`.

G3 does not:
- copy product descriptions or ProductConfiguration as competing master data;
- infer product type from codes;
- create Article identities;
- own BOM or Routing definitions.

## Ordering and identity
Line identity is the UUID, not the sequence number.

The sequence number is unique within a Taquilla and may be corrected while the Taquilla remains DRAFT without changing line identity.

This preserves stable downstream references while allowing controlled pre-release editing.

## Decimal semantics
Requested quantities are persisted as:
`NUMERIC(24,8)`

Quantities must be strictly positive.

No float conversion is introduced at the persistence/service contract.

## Source idempotency
`source_line_key` is optional and unique inside the Taquilla when present.

Replay behavior:
- same Taquilla + source_line_key + same Article/sequence/quantity → existing line returned;
- same Taquilla + source_line_key with different content → conflict;
- no source_line_key → normal operator-created line identity.

This supports future G3.6 assimilation without turning source keys into canonical business identity.

## Lifecycle freeze
Composition may be created or corrected only while the parent Taquilla is `DRAFT`.

Once the Taquilla becomes `READY`, `COMPLETED` or `CANCELLED`, G3.2 composition is frozen.

This prevents silent demand mutation after release to downstream planning.
