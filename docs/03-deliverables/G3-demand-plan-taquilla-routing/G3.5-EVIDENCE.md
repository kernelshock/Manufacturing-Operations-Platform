## Historical administrative completion
A non-terminal Production Plan with at least one Taquilla can be closed through the dedicated historical-completion operation.

Required classification:
- `ON_TIME`;
- `DELAYED`.

Optional:
- administrative note.

Recorded evidence:
- classification;
- note;
- recording timestamp;
- recording actor.

The Plan becomes COMPLETED and each non-cancelled child Taquilla receives the same historical classification.

Previously cancelled Taquillas remain CANCELLED and are not rewritten as historical completion.

## Explicit MES boundary
Historical completion is an administrative fact only.

It does **not** create or infer:
- operation start timestamps;
- operation finish timestamps;
- production quantities actually achieved;
- scrap;
- resource usage;
- shop-floor events;
- MES execution trace.

Audit evidence explicitly includes:
`mes_actuals_created = false`.

The `historical_completed_at` field is the timestamp when the administrative classification was recorded, not a manufacturing completion timestamp.

## Live versus historical completion
Historical completion cannot overwrite a Taquilla already marked COMPLETED through the normal lifecycle.

This avoids silently reclassifying a live/operational completion as imported historical evidence.

Normal Production Plan completion is now stricter:
- the Plan must be OPEN;
- it must contain at least one Taquilla;
- every Taquilla must already be COMPLETED or CANCELLED.

## Governed cancellation
Generic lifecycle transitions to CANCELLED were removed from the ordinary status-transition maps.

Dedicated cancellation operations require an explicit reason.

Taquilla cancellation:
- allowed only while non-terminal;
- preserves UUID and business identity;
- records reason, actor and recording timestamp.

Plan cancellation:
- allowed only while non-terminal;
- rejected when any child Taquilla is already COMPLETED;
- cascades the same administrative cancellation evidence to non-terminal child Taquillas.

## Safe correction / deletion
A Taquilla may be hard-deleted only when:
- parent Plan is non-terminal;
- Taquilla status is DRAFT;
- there is no dependent product composition.

If product composition exists, the Taquilla must be cancelled rather than deleted.

This implements the operator need to remove a truly erroneous, unused draft entry while preserving referenced history once meaningful demand evidence exists.
