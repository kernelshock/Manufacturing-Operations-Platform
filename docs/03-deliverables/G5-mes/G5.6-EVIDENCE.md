## API
- `GET /api/v1/mes/feedback/operations/{operation_id}`
- `GET /api/v1/mes/feedback/dispatches/{dispatch_id}`

Both require `mes.read`. There are no write endpoints.

## Classification
- NOT_STARTED: no actual start/finish.
- IN_PROGRESS: RUNNING or PAUSED; at dispatch level also incomplete execution or unresolved rework.
- ON_TIME: explicitly completed and actual finish at or before the published planned finish.
- DELAYED: explicitly completed and actual finish after published planned finish.

Dispatch ON_TIME requires **every** operation completed on time and no open rework. Otherwise completed dispatch is DELAYED if any operation finishes late. A dispatch with no operations is never completed. Incomplete dispatches are NOT_STARTED or IN_PROGRESS; missing actuals are unknown rather than inferred. Aggregate classification is **operation compliance**, not administrative G3 plan/taquilla completion.

## Projection
Explicit operation identifiers, Plan, Taquilla, ProductLine, routing provenance, publication fingerprint, actual/planned times, start/finish offset in seconds, quantity delta as Decimal, actual-resource deviation, rework count and whether manual replan review is recommended.

A recommendation is **not** a mutation, replan, publication, automatic delay cause or completion action. Open rework and documented quantity shortfall request manual review.

## Scope boundaries / known limitations
- No wall-clock-based forecast of currently late unfinished work in this slice.
- No automatic root-cause attribution, material truth, maintenance authority or employee attendance.
- Quantity variance is independent of on-time performance: ON_TIME does not imply full demanded quantity.
- Per-operation classification uses published operation finish as baseline. The dispatch aggregate is not a substitute for a customer due-date or G3 administrative completion judgment.
- No mandatory schema migration; projection reads existing G5.1–G5.5 data.
- CI and full PostgreSQL integration not yet asserted here.
