## Implemented trace

- **G6.7 backend:** `src/mop/materials/projection_service.py`, `projection_schemas.py`; endpoints `GET /api/v1/materials/projection/plants`, `/plans`, `/plans/{plan_id}?plant_id=...&effective_at=...`, and separately permissioned `/plans/{plan_id}/procurement`.
- **G6.7 frontend:** `frontend/src/shell/MaterialsProjectionPanel.tsx` and `.css`; navigation in `AppShell.tsx` and `lib/permissions.ts`. Plan/plant/BOM-effective-date selectors; synthetic read-only dashboard; article filters, detail per location, CSV export, print stylesheet and risk/unknown semantics.
- **Integration test:** `tests/g6/test_materials_projection_integration.py` checks mixed multilevel plan aggregate (cancelled taquilla excluded); reserved-for-plan vs total reservations; physical inventory not double-decremented by measured consumption; UNKNOWN stock when an active location has no opening; plant-isolated ledger; no projection-side writes; verified Prolowin evidence in separate RBAC boundary; forbidden access without `prolowin.procurement.read`.
- **Inventory and demand boundaries:** G2 published BOM + G3 plan/taquilla create theoretical requirements; G6.2 signed physical ledger creates on-hand; G6.5 reservation ledger creates commitments; G6.4 actual measured consumption is displayed as a separate evidence channel, already reflected in physical stock after the explicit ISSUE. An analytic read NEVER posts an ISSUE, HOLD, receipt, purchase order, supplier change or Quality disposition.
- **Supply evidence:** G6.6 verified manual samples only. Missing evidence cannot be interpreted as no outstanding orders, no claims or on-time supply. Original and revised promises remain independent; Excel weekly snapshot is not an authoritative event feed. No inferred purchase/physical arrival.
- **Displayed gross gap:** `max(0, BOM gross requirement - reservations for this plan - max(0, unallocated physical))` only with known stock. This is a *gross indicative exposure* and **NOT** ATP, purchase recommendation, date-adjusted net requirements or a completed-production variance. Reservations for other plans reduce unallocated physical. Unknown location opening produces UNKNOWN, not fabricated zero.
