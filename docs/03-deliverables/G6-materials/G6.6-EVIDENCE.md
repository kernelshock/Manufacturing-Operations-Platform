## Operating assumption

FAIC confirmed Prolowin is a closed, outdated program. Access to its API/DB is unconfirmed and Excel/CSV exports may be inadequate. **G6.6 must operate completely without direct Prolowin connection or stable export formats.** An operator can transcribe a source screen, optionally attach an original screenshot, and obtain an independent review. OCR and reliable automated screenshot parsing are NOT claimed.

## Verified flows in implemented code

- **Source evidence:** manual-source note, screenshot upload as PNG/JPEG (max 4 MiB, enforced during streaming), SHA-256 evidence fingerprint and immutable source bytes in PostgreSQL, with auth-protected download and no-store caching. Weekly Excel snapshot permitted as **secondary evidence only** and blocked from independently establishing a VERIFIED transaction.
- **Typed procurement transcription:** `ORDER`, `RECEIPT` (partial deliveries and optional, separately evidenced Quality-accepted quantity), `RESCHEDULE` (only with confirmed absence of production risk and documented reason), `CLAIM`, `ESCALATION` to evaluate alternatives. Original promise is immutable; any revised date remains independent. Prolowin order/line, source supplier/article/UoM codes, document reference, date, actor and source capture are preserved.
- **Two-person governance:** permissions `prolowin.procurement.read` / `capture` / `verify`, distinct transcriber/reviewer, explicit review decision/note and audit. Verified entries require unambiguous G2 supplier, article and canonical UoM matching and one previously verified original PO for subsequent events. A receipt exceeding ordered quantity cannot be accepted. Review changes status/mapping only; evidence payload is immutable by PostgreSQL trigger.
- **Deduplication:** source-event idempotency key is unique; additionally, a PostgreSQL partial unique index allows **only one VERIFIED** event for a given PO, line, kind and source document reference. Pending/rejected transcriptions remain visible, allowing correction without deleting audit history. Corrections to an already VERIFIED record will require an explicit supersession/invalidating process before being allowed to replace trusted analytics.
- **Trusted projection:** verified-only PO-line summary shows original/revised commitment dates, quantity ordered/delivered/accepted when documented, delivered-quantity outstanding, partial receipt count, claims and production-risk escalations, original-versus-revised **calendar-day** lateness and completion lead time. It carries a visible `PARTIAL_MANUAL_CAPTURE_NOT_ERP_SYNC` completeness indicator. These do NOT establish full ERP synchronization or business-day supplier reliability.
- **Strict side effects:** none of these intake events posts physical G6 stock, accepts goods for Quality, changes G2 BOM, modifies G5 execution, creates a purchase order, triggers supplier replacement or recalculates inventory reservations. Original Prolowin remains the primary procurement source of record.
- **Operator UI:** `Compras · Prolowin` navigation and `ProlowinCapturePanel.tsx` support source capture, image attachment and inspection by the reviewer, date-only transcription, event types, two-person verification/rejection and historic PO status. Human calendar dates use a normalized noon-UTC transport value; this is NOT an assertion about the physical receipt hour.
