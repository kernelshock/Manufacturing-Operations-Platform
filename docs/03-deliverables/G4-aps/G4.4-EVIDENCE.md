## Calendar continuity

Operations can span multiple availability intervals.

A non-working interval such as lunch does not consume capacity. The operation retains one overall planned start/finish envelope and separate productive segments around the break.

Validated integration example:
- operation 1 starts 11:45 local;
- productive segment 11:45–12:00;
- lunch 12:00–12:30 is not scheduled;
- productive segment resumes 12:30–12:45;
- operation 1 planned finish is 12:45;
- operation 2 on the same ProductLine starts no earlier than 12:45 and finishes 13:15;
- resource segments do not overlap.

## Boundaries preserved

G4.4 owns planned timing only.

It does not introduce:
- actual_start / actual_finish;
- actual produced quantity;
- scrap;
- operator execution;
- MES trace;
- schedule publication/supersession;
- frontend Gantt;
- material, maintenance or workforce constraints from unreleased later gates.
